## LUDO: BASE GAME
### OBJECTIVE
Ludo is a classic board game played with dice and pawns. The goal is to be the first player to move all four of your pawns from the starting area (Safe Zone) to your home area by rolling the dice and moving strategically around the board.
•	Number of players: Ludo is designed for 2 to 4 players.

### BEGINNING
Each player starts with four pawns in their starting area (the circle of their color with 4 empty spaces inside).
TURN
At the beginning of each turn, the player draws 3 cards out of a Dice Card Pool. He then chooses one and rolls the die. The 3 cards are shuffled back into the Dice Card Pool.
When he rolls the highest number of his chosen die, he is allowed to move one of their pawns out of the starting area onto their start square. Alternatively, he is able to move one of his existing pieces forward along the track.

### ELIMINATING PAWNS
•	If your pawn lands exactly on a square occupied by an opponent’s pawn, you “capture” or eliminate that pawn. The opponent’s pawn is sent back to its starting area.

## PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The goal is to implement an advanced Ludo alternative, where the player has more options than just rolling dice. Ludo Advanced will cover one Dice Card Pool and one Skill Card Pool.

### DICE CARD POOL
The Dice Card Pool includes multiple cards ranging from D2-D20.

### SKILL CARD POOL
The Skill Card Pool includes cards with different effect types:
•	Action: Activatable in your own turn.
•	Reaction: Activatable in response to actions of other players.
