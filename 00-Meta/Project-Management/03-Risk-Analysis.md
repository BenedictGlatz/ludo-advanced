## Risks added 2026-08-22 by the project plan (issue #15)

Kept as their own block rather than merged into the table above, so that the report can show which
risks the register held before the plan and which the plan itself created. A plan that commits to a
date and a sequence adds risk by doing so; these five are that risk, and section 5.3 of
[Project-Plan.md](Project-Plan.md) is where each is argued.

| Risk | Category | Likelihood | Impact | Priority | Mitigation / Response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Feature freeze of 2026-09-11 missed and the closing window eaten | Schedule | H | H | 5 | **The highest-rated risk in this register.** It follows from the required rate: 74 must-have points over the 15 implementation weekdays the plan leaves is 4.9 points per weekday, and the scale is unvalidated because no issue has ever been closed with points recorded. Response: milestone M3 on 2026-09-06. If a full turn does not resolve by then, scope is cut with the Product Owner rather than the freeze being moved, because the closing window holds the playtest, the fallback video, the deck and the report and none of those can absorb a slip. |
| Critical path stalls on one person | Schedule | M | H | 4 | Section 4.3 of [Project-Plan.md](Project-Plan.md): the critical path is 46 of the 74 must-have points, and #27 turn manager (8) plus #33 card effects (13) are 21 points that do not split between two people because each is the integration point the others depend on. Only 32 points of work exist off the path, so the second implementer runs dry before the first finishes it. Response: pair on #27 and #33, front-load the 32 parallel points into Sprint 2, and split #28 so its animation half becomes parallel work. |
| No date after 2026-09-17 is known to exist | Schedule | M | H | 4 | Every date in the project plan assumes 2026-09-17 because that is the last date the board carries. The module's actual deadline is recorded nowhere in this repository, which is part of the standing unknown-requirements gap in [00-index.md](../Documentation/00-index.md). Response: ask. It is one question to one person and it changes every figure in the plan, in either direction. |
| The Sprint 2 and Sprint 3 assignment never reaches the board | Process/Quality | H | M | 4 | Section 4.4 of [Project-Plan.md](Project-Plan.md) assigns all 27 unscheduled issues, and setting the `Sprint` field needs the `project` token scope the `gh` token does not carry. So the assignment exists as a document and not as board state, which is the exact shape of the gap that hid Sprint 1's whole gameplay scope until 2026-08-22. Response: one interactive `gh auth refresh -s project`, which also unblocks the `Story Points` field and moving cards. Three blocked outcomes, one human action. |
| The story point scale is wrong in one direction | Schedule | M | M | 3 | The estimates come from one estimator and one AI session, not a planning poker round, and no item has been built to check one against. Response: record points against the first three issues closed in Sprint 2 and re-check the totals then, not at the end, since a scale error found in September is a finding and one found in August is a correction. |
